1. Aesthetic (Is the visualization pleasing to look at?)
    * this visualization is pleasing to look at, but difficult and convoluted to accurately decipher
    * gradient pastel colour stars do not have high contrast and blend in with each other
    * sentences are cut off under Star Size & Distance sections
2. Substantive (Does the visualization accurately represent data?)
    * this visualization does not accurately represent data:
    * there is no x-axis or y-axis and thus, no way of comparing impact of movie stars
    * Star Size (# of Films): the "0" or origin point is at the centre, and there is no actual axis which makes it very confusing to compare sizes because you cannot judge numerical size of stars, only the approximate size of stars.
    * Color (Hit Score): gradient of pastel pink for low impact and pastel blue for high impact is again an approximate way to measure as there's no numerical axis
    * Distance (Diversity): again there is no numerical axis with which to measure the diversity, only approximate distance judged by the naked eye
3. Perceptual (Can we understand what message the maker of the visualization is attempting to convey?)
    * I cannot understand what the maker is trying to convey
    * Heavy intrinsic & extraneous cognitive load: Readers will spend more time trying to understand how to read the visualization rather than the actual message
    * the pastel stars are "Influence Scores" which is a composite score of 3 variables but since there is no axis, nor distinct numerical categories, readers cannot understand the visualization.
4. How could this data visualization have been improved?  
    * decrease cognitive load by:
        - use familiar chart types rather than rare
        - use accurate (aboslute) interpretation
        - use more concise and less detailed composition (less visual elements)
        - use explanatory composition rather than exploratory (a chart that readers are guided through step-by-step)
    * non-gradient, higher contrast & non-pastel colours
        - eg. rainbow colours - red: IMDB score 0 - 1.0, orange: IMDB score 1.0 - 2.0, yellow: IMDB score 2.0 - 3.0
    * consider using x-axis & y-axis and not as many variables
    * clearer legend so readers aren't confused
    * set distinct star sizes for number of films
        - eg. star size 1: 0-20 films -> star size 5: 100+ films
